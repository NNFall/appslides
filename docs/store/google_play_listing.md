# Google Play Listing Text

## App Name

Slide AI

## Short Description

Create AI presentations and convert PDF, DOCX and PPTX files.

## Full Description

Slide AI helps you create presentations and work with documents in a simple chat-style interface.

Enter a topic, choose the number of slides and select a design. The app generates an editable PowerPoint presentation and a PDF version for viewing or sharing.

Main features:

- AI presentation generation by topic
- Editable PPTX output
- PDF export
- PDF, DOCX and PPTX conversion tools
- Template-based slide designs
- Local chat-style workflow
- Subscription-based generation limits

How it works:

1. Write the topic of your presentation.
2. Choose the number of slides.
3. Review and confirm the outline.
4. Select a design template.
5. Receive PPTX and PDF files.

The app is designed for students, teachers, business users and anyone who needs to prepare presentation drafts faster.

Subscriptions are processed through Google Play. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Google Play account settings.
